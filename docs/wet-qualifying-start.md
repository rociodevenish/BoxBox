# Wet qualifying and starting tyres, version 1

Weather and WetTyres must both be explicitly configured by the server to enable this flow. Ordinary live presets remain dry. The existing test-only `F1_WET_TYRE_FIXTURE=1` now also supplies its configuration to preparation through `ApplyWeekendConditions`; the actual selected circuit stamps WeatherInputs provenance. Each setup revision and automatic preparation preserves this explicit capability in its effective configuration. Effective Cars remain unchanged and rebuild validation remains active.

`qualifying-attempt-v2` records `qualifying-common-interval-v1`: Q1, Q2 and Q3 each occupy an authored 180-second abstract interval, offset from the frozen Weather.TimeOriginSeconds. Every eligible driver's one abstract attempt uses the interval midpoint (origin +90/+270/+450 seconds). Human submission order, decision duration, GETs and retries cannot choose a different condition. There is no qualifying compound/run-management choice. The same CircuitWeather process supplies midpoint observations and team forecasts. Observed conditions are public; only the team's controller/Open-authorised projection receives its uncertain forecast. Forecast identities are deterministic functions of that recorded observation and saved forecast tuning, never a hidden future path.

The wet abstraction adds at most 10 seconds/lap at saturation: six authored seconds plus the existing bounded WetSkillSeconds coefficient scaled by detailed wet skill and effective traction. It scales the existing single qualifying error opportunity by at most two. No second error draw is introduced; `qualifying-keyed-v1` remains the named channel. These are game approximation hypotheses. Legacy qualifying-attempt-v1 retains its prior timing and randomness; null Conditions is omitted in saved JSON. Reads replay every stored attempt with its exact model, rules, randomness and recorded midpoint conditions before exposing the saved grid.

After wet Q3, the complete 22-driver grid and results commit with lifecycle `starting-tyres`. No race engine exists yet. All drivers, including eliminated qualifiers, receive one starting-compound choice. Existing authenticated qualifying controller routes and SignalR subscriptions carry the window. POST `/api/lobbies/{id}/qualifying/teams/{teamId}/starting-tyres?deviceId=...` accepts StartingTyresCommand with CommandId, Revision, AssignmentRevision and complete `{entryId, compound}` choices. The five full compound names are legal only with the explicit wet flow. This typed command is separate from qualifying approaches and introduces no launch strategy.

The window freezes observation-only AI and timeout choices before accepting a human lock: Medium below .25 observed wetness, Intermediate below .7, otherwise Wet (`starting-tyres-observation-v1`). Its threshold descriptions are a coarse disclosed suitability summary, not a forecast or calibrated crossover claim. Own-team fallbacks are displayed. The timing policy includes the existing shared-device allowance. A saved deadline, generation, remaining pause time, all-team readiness and idempotent command receipts reuse the durable qualifying module. Accepted human locks survive refresh, pause, retry and restart; timeout fills only missing locks. Ticket 05 may refine the frozen observation policy and assistance without changing accepted choices.

The opening uses the same process seed/source and begins at absolute origin +540 seconds. Accepted compounds are a separate immutable StartingCompounds map in `automatic-standing-start-v3`; they never rewrite frozen effective Cars. StartingDrivers applies the map and preserves effective WetWeather attributes. Wet skill adds a bounded launch grip loss through the existing launch ability calculation, with no additional random opportunity. The existing RaceEngine drives weather-sensitive opening travel and incident exposure. Legacy automatic-standing-start-v1 remains Conserve and v2 remains Standard with omitted null compound map and unchanged dry channels.

Reviewed provisional inputs remain driver/track schema 1.0.0 / 2026.10.08-initial-balance and car specification 1.0.0 / 2026.10.08-initial-model. Detailed wetWeatherSkill and setup-adjusted traction are finite 1-100 design points via wet-effective-v1; no headline stars become physics. The existing temperature-management/thermal/degradation mapping is unchanged. Existing stable driver/track identity text, portrait and circuit manifests/provenance and fallbacks are reused. The asset collection still has the same two image directories and 51 originals; no image or geometric conversion is introduced.

When the server explicitly supplies WeatherReactions, preparation also preserves that capability.
