# Preparation guidance v1

`PreparationDecisionPolicy.Evaluate(PreparationPolicyObservation)` is the public deterministic simulation/policy boundary. Its explicit input contains versioned circuit demands, own base-car design scores, both drivers' named preferences and Adaptability, and optionally their saved legitimate practice observations. It accepts no exact DriverFits, effective attributes, practice diagnostics, replay seed, opponents or future outcomes. `SessionCoordinator` constructs this observation by selecting those fields explicitly; it never passes the complete assessment result to the policy.

The authored policy version is `preparation-observation-v1`, with input schema `preparation-observation-input-v1`. These are tuning hypotheses, not calibrated pace optimization. Aero uses a moderate Speed bias when mean straight demand exceeds mean corner demand by more than five design points, Downforce bias for the opposite difference, otherwise Balanced. The existing preference-to-Mechanical mapping is shared with setup compatibility; the policy computes the two drivers' preference compromise weighted by `125 - Adaptability`, rounded to a legal -2..2 choice. Lower Adaptability therefore carries more weight without inventing another preference attribute. Tyres uses Preservation bias at degradation demand 65+, Warm-Up bias below 55, otherwise Balanced. Own base-car scores explain the context; v1 does not optimize against exact pace or hidden effective values.

The final recommendation uses the one saved assessment. Moderate/High-confidence observed preference mismatch permits one Mechanical step toward the shared preference compromise; Low-confidence feedback or no mismatch retains the assessed Mechanical choice. Both drivers' observed fit, confidence and uncertainty are quoted as information, never as qualifying/race predictions. All five choices on every axis remain available, and all setup compromises still apply.

Beginning preparation computes each unclaimed team's provisional recommendation, records that observation and choice, assesses it once, then records the final observation/recommendation using its own feedback and marks it final-ready. Original preset/content versions, complete practice inputs/result and final effective inputs stay preserved alongside both recorded choices. Source is `unclaimed-ai`; policy and observation/source versions are recorded. This entire batch is part of the existing single durable BeginPreparation mutation. Retrying BeginPreparation or restarting reuses those records rather than reassessing. Assigned human teams remain provisional and are never overwritten by automatic preparation. The ticket 04 full-field freeze includes these recorded automatic choices in each frozen team context. Actual qualifying integration remains S08.

Private guidance uses the currently assigned player's existing Standard/Assisted/Guided settings. Standard omits recommendations; Assisted/Guided explain circuit demand, both drivers, uncertainty and compromises. Changing help settings publishes the saved private projection through existing revision-qualified household groups and intentional Open-family groups. Normal public preparation exposes readiness only. Automatic replay records are available solely through the existing loopback/configured-designer-key guard at `/api/designer/lobbies/{id}/preparation`.

The controller copies a recommendation into its unsaved draft only after the player presses **Use recommended setup**. Rendering advice, changing help, enabling the existing autoaccept setting or reconnecting does not submit or confirm preparation. The existing autoaccept setting applies at race decision expiry; S07 has no preparation timeout. Same-authority help updates preserve drafts. Reconnection hides private content, rechecks current authority and restores an editable draft only when team, assignment revision and saved team revision match. Handoff, revocation or changed authority clears private content.

The existing supplied driver/track identities, portraits, fallbacks and provisional source versions remain unchanged. This ticket adds no imagery, card-star conversion, AI acquisition/development, extra practice budget or S08 mechanics. Focused policy/API/SignalR/browser evidence is recorded in ticket 05; player comprehension and hosted acceptance remain unverified.
