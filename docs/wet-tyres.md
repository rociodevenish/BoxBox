# Wet tyres, version 1

`RaceConfiguration.WetTyres` explicitly enables `wet-tyres-v1` and `actual-race-wet-use-v1`. It is independent of `Weather`: observing weather alone does not change physics or unlock wet controls. Null retains historical dry inputs, JSON, random channels and replay identities. Soft/Medium/Hard keep ordinals 0/1/2; Intermediate/Wet append 3/4. Wet selections require explicit tuning, including through Safety Car choices. Replay model names append both effect and eligibility versions; randomness retains the existing weather/error channels without adding another accident draw.

The authored suitability curve uses wetness [0,1]. Dry unsuitability is wetness; Wet unsuitability is 1−wetness; Intermediate is normalized distance from its configurable optimum (default .45). Squared unsuitability produces continuous, bounded wrong-condition pace (default maxima 26/20/24 seconds per lap for dry/Intermediate/Wet) and extra wear (default maximum +3 times ordinary wear). Intermediate/Wet base wear multipliers default 1.05/1.1 and warm-up multipliers .8/1.1, applied through existing degradation-per-lap and warm-up-seconds tuning. No puncture, disqualification, inventory or service-time rule is created by unsuitable tyres. Sets remain unlimited and lane/service accounting is unchanged. These numbers are tuning hypotheses, not a balance or meteorological claim.

`WetWeatherAttributes` contains detailed effective Wet-Weather Skill, Tyre Temperature Management and Traction, each finite 1–100 design points. `WeekendPreparation.EffectiveWetWeather` reads driver `wetWeatherSkill` directly and car `tyreTemperature`/`traction` after the existing setup projection; it never derives physics from stars. Source provenance includes `wet-effective-v1`, driver card `1.0.0/2026.10.08-initial-balance`, car `car-spec/1.0.0/2026.10.08-initial-model` and setup version. The current setup changes traction by three points per Mechanical choice and leaves temperature capability unchanged. Existing thermal-demand-weighted TyreManagement and degradation-demand-weighted preservation remain their existing independent inputs. Reviewed construction menu `1.0.0/2026.10.09-preseason-car-build-v1` is not newly activated by this slice.

Temperature management reduces wrong-condition pace/wear by at most 15%. Lower wet skill/traction add at most the configured four seconds per lap at saturation; dry weather removes this term. Wetness, suitability and those two attributes multiply the **existing integrated error exposure** once, with a bounded configurable coefficient (default 2); mechanical hazards, severity identities and contact opportunities remain the existing channels. Neutralised running still suppresses error exposure. There is no weather accident roll.

## Time integration

Authoritative observed conditions always come directly from `CircuitWeather.Observe(tuning, seed, TimeOriginSeconds + raceTime)`. The numerical effect model uses fixed absolute-time one-second semantic cells, additionally split at actual rain-process interval knots. Each cell samples the continuous suitability curve at its left edge, holds pace/wear/error coefficients fixed until the next knot, consumes distance-dependent wear/exposure under the old coefficients, then rebuilds remaining trajectories at the new coefficients. This is an explicit first-order approximation of continuously evolving effects, not exact continuous ODE integration. Equivalent segment subdivision and diagnostic reads cannot choose different sampling points. Fractional absolute clock origins and rain intervals are supported. Strategic crossing/reaction windows are a later slice and must use this same clock and process.

Lap-entry tyre pace/degradation inputs remain frozen between existing instruction/lap updates; weather cell rebuilds do not resample degradation by segment. Existing committed pit stops, incident time losses and persistent damage survive cell rebuilds. Reads do not consume wear, advance hazard exposure or update condition cells. Safety Car travel retains its prescribed speed and consumes weather-sensitive wear through the same clock.

## Eligibility and projection

The two-dry-compound rule is waived only after a driver travels positive race distance on Intermediate or Wet. A queued stop, a newly fitted set still in service, a forecast, wet qualifying and the starting grid alone do not qualify. Dry use retains its existing recorded-compound behavior. Raw classification and eligibility remain separate; no corrective stop is forced. The rule version is recorded with the replay model; historical raw outcome serialization is unchanged when wet tuning is null.

Public/team/open snapshots carry nullable `WetTyresEnabled=true` only for explicit tuning; null is omitted for legacy sessions. It grants compound controls, not hidden suitability, physical condition, attributes, risk exposure or future path access. Both-car controls provide full compound labels on phones, observed/predicted weather remain distinct, and the public display has no private forecast. Live presets remain disabled until the complete wet-weekend flow is integrated. `F1_WET_TYRE_FIXTURE=1` enables only the real API browser fixture.
