# Weather assistance and recovery, version 1

`weather-observation-ai-v1` accepts only `TeamObservation`, public timing, the explicit wet-compound capability and observed Safety Car control. Its input has no weather tuning, seed, future path, engine or diagnostic state. Changing hidden future weather while preserving these observations leaves its complete output unchanged. Weather presence alone does not enable wet choices. Dry sessions retain `dry-observation-ai-v2`; legacy opponent versions remain readable.

The deliberately coarse current suitability policy uses Medium below 25% observed wetness, Intermediate below 70%, otherwise Wet. These authored summaries are independent of reaction-window hysteresis thresholds and are not calibrated tyre crossovers. Dry compounds already fitted count as suitable in the dry band. It considers fresh tyres below the disclosed 38% condition estimate, and preserves the two-dry-compound requirement unless actual wet-compound use is observed. It never replaces a queued or ongoing stop. Standard pace and Neutral battle are conservative defaults; active Safety Car control restricts the legal choices to Conserve and Neutral. Retired/finished drivers receive no new instruction.

Ordinary race AI, weather-window fallback and Assisted/Guided race advice share that policy. Advice records its version, current observation time, concrete choices, reasons, uncertainty and complete legal options before locks open. Confidence never narrows uncertain choices. Explicit recommendation acceptance changes only an unsubmitted team's timeout choice; human locks remain authoritative. Standard omits the strategic recommendation from its private projection, while retaining the disclosed fallback and uncertain team forecast. Assisted and Guided show the saved both-car plan; Guided explains why options remain available. The policy does not claim that predicted rain determines what will happen before a stop.

Preparation saves the current observation and each team's forecast with the prepared weekend. Setup/practice revisions retain them. Qualifying uses the previously recorded common midpoint observations and frozen tuning; starting tyres additionally save the forecast and help settings at the opening of the tyre window. Starting guidance shows the same observation-only fallback, explains uncertainty at launch and keeps all five compound options available. The existing typed starting-compound locks and immutable `automatic-standing-start-v3` compound map/time origin are unchanged. Race forecasts reconstruct from recorded simulation state and frozen forecast inputs; repeated reads, retries and recovery have the same observation identity and probability ranges.

Forecast confidence, probability ranges and suggested plans remain own-team information, including intentional Open views with their existing authority rules. Current conditions, weather-trigger identities, observed incident facts and concrete recorded tyre-change events are public. The public board prioritises a concise observed weather/tyre decision fact, combines coincident Safety Car causes in one banner, and carries mandatory control/committed-stop facts in its event panel. It contains no forecast confidence or probabilities. Controller text keeps the full legal policy. Reduced motion uses the existing immediate feedback path.

The durable record still commits before any advanced result is broadcast. Stored pending operations, frozen automatic choices, accepted command receipts and typed Weather outcomes reconstruct exactly once. Real Firestore fault checks exercise both a failed write before commit and a lost acknowledgement after commit for starting locks and mixed weather/Safety Car outcomes. Historical S09 disruption staging v1/actual v2 and S08 opening v1 Conserve/v2 Standard dispatch remain unchanged.

Content remains driver/track schema 1.0.0 / 2026.10.08-initial-balance, car specification 1.0.0 / 2026.10.08-initial-model and the existing `wet-effective-v1` detailed mappings. No card stars become policy or physics inputs. The current supplied collection contains two image folders and 51 originals. Existing stable portrait/circuit manifests, hashes, provenance and packaged variants remain unchanged; live names, full compound labels and missing-image fallbacks remain available. Advice headings receive the controller's live driver names. No image-derived geometry or new assets are introduced.

All new live flows remain behind the existing explicit capabilities and test-only fixture gates. `F1_WEATHER_ADVICE_FIXTURE=1` is available only in the real browser fixture host. Its mixed preset aligns one observed mechanical interruption and one weather edge using a public simulation probe with zero weather physical effects; it is a correctness fixture, not balance evidence or a production preset. Whole-weekend duration, forecast understanding and tyre crossover calibration remain ticket 06 evidence.
