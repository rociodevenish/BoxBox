# Weather reaction windows, version 1

`RaceConfiguration.WeatherReactions` explicitly enables `weather-reactions-v1`; both Weather and WetTyres are required. Null omits all new configuration/result/public fields and leaves historical replay model names and outcome hashes unchanged. The authored default suitability boundaries are wetness .25 and .70, hysteresis .03 and minimum simulated separation 30 seconds. These are tunable hypotheses, independent of the continuous tyre effect curves and supplied card stars.

The remembered Dry/Intermediate/Wet band advances only at its outward hysteresis edge: rising .28/.73 or falling .67/.22 by default. Initial wetness selects the initial band without a window. Rain is constant inside each existing weather interval, so the exact linear wetness crossing is an earliest-event candidate alongside car events, the ordinary phase target, the neutralisation deadline and the existing one-second absolute-time effect cells. If minimum separation delays a crossing, the first still-applicable time after that separation is used. Rain knots are drained before searching another interval. Reads never move the clock or change the band.

All events at a chosen timestamp drain before commands open. Finish takes precedence and hides every strategic trigger. Otherwise coincident incident and weather causes form one Weather operation/window, then an ordinary boundary; a weather trigger at a neutralisation deadline opens after that control ends. Weather has its own typed `WeatherReaction` result and optional public projection, with observed conditions, band, identity, causes and neutralisation facts. It is never encoded as an IncidentKind. The existing incident projection remains an actual observed incident only. No future weather path, process seed, hazard exposure or private forecast enters the public extension.

`ResolveWeatherReaction` atomically requires complete Pace/Pit/contextual Battle choices for exactly the active cars, including cars with queued/ongoing stops. A new pit still enters at the driver's next lap-end. Stay Out preserves an existing commitment; replacement and duplicate pit requests fail before mutation. Under active or coincident Safety Car control Conserve and Neutral are the only legal Pace/Battle choices, disclosed in the UI and validated by the public engine/session command. Neutralisation retains actual remaining travel, lane/service accounting and its original absolute deadline across subsequent weather windows; it never restarts the period. Pure weather offers the ordinary legal Pace and contextual Battle set.

The session uses the existing complete TeamCommand with `WindowKind="Weather"`, existing accepted-command identities, simultaneous lock barrier, shared-device allowance, pause/deadline machinery and commit-before-broadcast storage. Accepted operations, observed trigger and concrete frozen plans reconstruct deterministically through BrowserReplay's Weather dispatch. Existing disruption-v1 staging and disruption-v2 SafetyCar dispatch retain their independent meanings. Typed weather exports use disruption-v2 with the explicit new simulation model suffix; old null-weather exports retain their original format/hash.

The temporary fallback freezes only public current wetness and own legal car state: Medium/Intermediate/Wet by current suitability band, Standard in racing or mandatory Conserve under control, Neutral, and a new compound only for an uncommitted car. Ticket 05 owns complete weather assistance and AI. This slice introduces no perfect forecast, hidden path access or new advice claims. Live presets remain disabled; `F1_WEATHER_REACTION_FIXTURE=1` exists only in the real API browser test host.

The controller and public display reuse the 51 supplied original driver/track images and existing stable portrait mappings, runtime variants and live fallback text. No new imagery or geometry is created. Source versions/mappings remain those recorded in circuit-weather.md and wet-tyres.md: driver/track 1.0.0/2026.10.08-initial-balance and car-spec 1.0.0/2026.10.08-initial-model. Wet-weather skill and tyre-temperature capability retain ticket 02's detailed effective mappings; weather thresholds are separately authored tuning.
