Validation occurs before the campaign reservation or lobby creation. Bad references, inconsistent source versions or unsupported consumers, invalid group labels/units/bounds, invalid travel times, insufficient pools and unaffordable completion fail as InvalidInput; retrying that creation identity with a corrected deployment remains possible because no record was reserved.

Recovery/retries do not reopen the mutable deployment catalogue. Legacy versionless source-labelled snapshots remain compatible when their stored consumer/model contracts validate. An explicitly selected unsupported candidate version fails actionably without overwriting saved data. Missing compatible deployment catalogue entries do not invalidate an already-frozen supported campaign.
