# S11 ticket 02 — points, committed results and standings

Answer: finished first-event results enter the campaign once through the application gateway and one campaign-record CAS. The gateway rereads the durable finished weekend, verifies its recorded deterministic replay, and projects an explicit public allowlist: frozen entry/person/team identities, raw position, laps/progress, gaps, finish/retirement time, eligibility and status. It never accepts a player-supplied classification and never serializes PhaseResult, decisions, tyres, BattleOptions, private preparation, or engine internals. Each immutable contribution retains session/storage revision, result hash, model/randomness and scoring/table versions. Reload validates references, hash, frozen model/distance and recalculated contribution; incompatible records are preserved and return Conflict.

`ChampionshipScoring` publishes authored `championship-scoring-v1` with configurable named game table, initially `game-points-25-18-v1`: 25/18/15/12/10/8/6/4/2/1 indexed by raw position. Retired or ineligible drivers receive zero and others are never promoted. No fastest-lap award. Creation freezes an optional configured table. Both drivers sum for constructors. Equal totals compare eligible, nonretired finish counts at every raw position (including 11th/12th and beyond), then the immutable roster starting order. Person IDs, rather than event entry IDs, accumulate points. Standings rebuild only from committed contributions. Concurrent coordinators, duplicate requests and uncertain before/after commit responses converge through CAS/reread. A late duplicate Start preserves committed-result lifecycle.

Authenticated campaign reads repair a missed result contribution after weekend completion; explicit `/commit-result?deviceId=…` is also idempotent. The minimal campaign page shows constructor/driver order, named scoring and tie rules, and raw event report with live driver names. Ticket 03 owns next-event progression/navigation; no second-event or season-completion behavior was added.
