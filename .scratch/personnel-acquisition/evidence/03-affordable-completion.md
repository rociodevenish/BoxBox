## Reserve and supply contract

`global-demand-envelope-v1` counts every unfilled mandatory place across all active teams separately for each role. With N remaining places of a role, its conservative price bound is the Nth cheapest **distinct unowned** person. Each team's Completion Reserve is the sum of these bounds for its own remaining places, plus the minimum viable car reserve. This cannot promise one cheap person to multiple places. Opening rejects content with too few unique identities or an envelope above starting credit, before creating any ledger or offer. The existing requirement for mandatory demand plus one spare (minimum three people per role) remains: final offers still have at least two distinct choices.

A purchase is legal only if its printed debit leaves the team's reserve **after that purchase**. It commits the same immutable debit, ownership, receipt, cursor and next offer transaction as before. Rejection changes nothing. Removing any one person while decreasing global demand by one cannot increase the Nth-price bound: the remaining N−1 cheapest people cost no more than the prior Nth person. Thus other teams' reserves cannot rise. The purchasing team's explicit check preserves its own budget. At least one remaining person costs at most the bound, and choosing that person always fits the reserve. Induction over the 77 saved picks establishes a legal continuation after every legal purchase, independently of policy, seed or Scout use; tests exercise expensive legal paths as well as cheap paths.

Each seeded draw keeps its normal two/three-card size. If its sampled cards contain no legal hire, one is replaced by a legal available person before persistence. Scout retains disjoint full-offer replacement and its existing insufficient-alternatives disclosure. It additionally refuses without spending a token when every disjoint alternative would violate the reserve. A single legal alternative is sufficient when enough total cards exist to replace the full offer; it does not require every replacement card to be affordable. No-cost reconnect never redraws. Validation rejects saved states with overspent reserves or offers containing no legal choice.

Private snapshots expose remaining slot identities, current reserve, readiness and a legal-action explanation/after-hire reserve for each **offered** card. They disclose no future pool identities or opponent budgets. Public snapshots add only `allRostersReady`. Seven filled places plus the retained car reserve makes the roster ready. Completion remains `acquiring` in this slice; the first lobby's pending-acquisition guard stays intact until ticket 04 applies acquired personnel to the frozen campaign and lobby. Accepted final-hire retries already replay after the cursor reaches 77. Ticket 04 must preserve receipt replay through its subsequent lifecycle transition, checking authority before receipts and receipts before rejecting the now-closed acquisition lifecycle.
