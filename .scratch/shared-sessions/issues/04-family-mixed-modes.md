# 04: Play in Family and Mixed privacy modes

**What to build:** A household chooses intentionally open TV decisions for designated teams while private teams keep their own information boundaries.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Lock multiple teams and resolve together.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Competitive mode uses private controllers; Family supports designated open teams; Mixed permits private, shared-tablet and open control per team.
- [ ] Lobby and active screens clearly identify intentional openness and distinguish spectator display access from an authorised TV controller assignment.
- [ ] Open views expose only the designated team's legitimate information and legal choices, never hidden diagnostics or another team's private estimates/selections.
- [ ] Authorised TV control submits the same atomic team command as private controllers; spectator displays cannot issue commands.
- [ ] Extend the Broadcast system with readable phone/tablet/TV privacy and ownership states and accessible labels.
- [ ] Verify open and Mixed browser flows and all public/private/open HTTP/SignalR projections, including competitive-team isolation.
