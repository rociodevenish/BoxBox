**What to build:** A player can choose instructions for both cars, review the complete team plan and understand its submitted or locked state in the gallery.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Build reusable driver cards and the executable gallery.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Distinct driver areas provide Conserve, Standard and Push; Stay Out or Pit with compound; and contextual Neutral, Attack and Defend.
- [ ] Short explanations describe instruction trade-offs; tyre condition is visibly an estimate in private fixtures.
- [ ] Both cars' selected choices appear in a clear confirmation summary before a fixture-driven submission/lock transition.
- [ ] Selected, submitted and locked states are distinct; loading, resolving, disabled and rejected-input states explain available actions and recovery.
- [ ] Contextual battle visibility and pending-stop restrictions are fixture inputs rather than duplicated game rules.
- [ ] Keyboard users can complete the flow with visible focus, and primary touch targets meet the agreed size and spacing rules.
- [ ] Review 360px and 768px portrait/landscape layouts, long names and errors through rendered interactions and inspected screenshots.
- [ ] Controller entry points do not import a race-rendering bundle; actual server validation and orchestration remain outside this ticket.
