# 03: Build the public timing tower

**What to build:** A viewer can inspect an accessible broadcast-style Classification with clear driver identity, gaps, tyres and stop status in the gallery.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Establish and validate the visual design system; 02 — Build reusable driver cards and the executable gallery.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Compose reusable row, identity, gap, compound and status treatments from the established visual rules.
- [ ] Inspect newly supplied track and other design assets for appropriate public-display context; use suitable assets within this ticket's scope, keep race facts as live text and retain accessible missing-asset fallbacks. Track-card and 3D-board implementation remain deferred.
- [ ] Show exact fixture Classification and distinguish time gaps, units and lap deficits, including dense 22-car fields and long names.
- [ ] Identify compounds with text as well as colour and show pending/ongoing stops without inventing unsupported race data.
- [ ] Public fixtures exclude private tyre estimates, hidden condition, random draws and opponent decisions.
- [ ] Inspect recognisable similarity against the dated broadcast references, recording adaptations for accessible cues, size and contrast.
- [ ] Review phone, tablet and 1920×1080 shared-display layouts; record screenshot dimensions, readability findings and limits of desktop review.
