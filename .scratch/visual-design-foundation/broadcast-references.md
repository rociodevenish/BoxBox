[Globo broadcaster footage preview](https://s04.video.glbimg.com/x720/14470151.jpg), linked from [Globo's Japanese GP practice coverage](https://ge.globo.com/motor/formula-1/noticia/2026/03/27/gp-do-japao-piastri-lidera-2o-treino-livre-bortoleto-e-16o.ghtml), published 2026-03-27. Observations below apply to that practice frame only.

The image was opened with `view_image`, not assessed through a search caption. Visible details:

- A tall translucent charcoal slab occupies roughly x=60–225 of a 1280-pixel-wide frame. All 22 rows fit between y≈118 and 690; rows are about 26 pixels high.
- Position, small team mark, bold three-letter driver code, timing/status, then small compound letter form consistent aligned columns.
- Timing uses white text such as `+0.026`; `Out Lap` and `No Time` occupy the timing column. White square `P` markers sit at the right on selected rows.
- `S`, `M`, and `H` appear in red, yellow, and pale white. The first position has a red tile; most position cells remain dark.
- The top block carries branding and a compact session clock. A separate lower timing card shows driver, lap time, and three colored sector indicators.

## Application judgment

Use the narrow column rhythm, aligned tabular numbers, terse identity, and restrained state accents as inspiration. Keep race gaps clearly labeled; this practice frame cannot establish race gap-versus-interval behavior, one-decimal precision, or animation. Give player selection its own explicit marker and preserve readable text for every color-coded state. Use project-owned typography, symbols, and colors rather than copying broadcast marks.
