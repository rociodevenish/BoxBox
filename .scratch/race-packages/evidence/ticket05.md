## Declaration and scope

The final matrix is seeds101–108 × two legal resource bundles × seven arms × two separately labelled scopes:224 logical runs and896 six-lap event-races. Shared minimum-car first-event prefixes reduce actual computations to736; the Base Car arm always simulates its own initial inputs. The sampling unit is eight paired seeds; logical car starts/retirements are correlated counterfactual observations.

Actual legal offers and completion reserve determine each roster. The target hires cheapest or highest-priced available legal offers; opponents choose cheapest actual offers. Price is not staff strength, and target acquisition can alter later offers/opponent rosters. All comparisons remain within the same bundle/scope/seed. Six arms confirm minimum cars. The seventh attempts the existing speed construction philosophy with its actual debit; failed builds retain their error and minimum fallback. Resources are equal before construction, not after paying for different cars.

`production-short` uses the supported Monza→Monaco shape with inactive default incidents and one opportunity. Small/large/refinement arms have exactly the same first package creation; large/refinement rows explicitly identify the small-library alias. No mature library/refinement claim is possible here.

`synthetic-alternating-six` repeats frozen Monza/Monaco three times with unique event/opportunity IDs, normal six-credit income after completed events and five opportunities. This is a separately authored public-boundary experiment, not an enabled production/full calendar. It freezes existing `disruption-live-opt-in-v1` hazards and reviewed circuit routes equally across siblings; no incidents are forced or rates increased. Reliability counts only with the actual active mechanical-failure consumer. Do not attribute cross-scope differences to package policy.

Fixed arms are minimum-car Save; relevant permanent work; Downforce/Low-drag small library then Save; five-template large library; paid Downforce then bounded strengthen/reduce/broaden/strengthen; the same five creation intents with Rush; and paid speed Base Car then Save. Pending parents cause explicit Save. Unaffordable/capped attempts retain exact errors and Save; Rush is never downgraded, resources never injected and purchased packages never supplied free. Actual library sizes, unused versions and retained credit are results, not assumptions. Selection records legitimate own-library/public-demand observation/recommendation/one completed version separately from project choices. See the tool README for full schedules, versions and race policy.

The original six-arm declaration was generated and exactly replayed at tool commit `b77ee478ba9eae4ec315e8bc856b31d7bc63ffd2`. Parent-spec review identified the missing direct Base Car comparison. Before running that additional arm, schema/policyv2 declared its fixed construction→Save control. Original schedules/resources/draws/race controls were preserved; the original v1 archive/manifest/summary remain separately preserved. This was scope completion, not an outcomes-based search.

Both scopes use a transparent constructor-order grid and fixed Hard-at-lap1 strategy. Race seeds and hidden opportunity/project/class identities match sibling arms; legitimate observations never receive future draws or opponent plans. This is not a full qualifying/AI/household campaign. Neither eight seeds nor repeated circuits establish optimal strategy, real calibration, full-calendar balance or runaway safety. Human understanding/workload (#74) remains unmeasured; validated full-calendar release comparisons remain deferred to S16.

The strongest observed opportunity-cost warning is affordability. In the production shape, none of16 Rush attempts can be funded after these legal rosters/minimum builds; expensive-legal hiring also leaves every permanent/package attempt unfunded. The two-credit extra speed Base Car is affordable for all eight economical rosters and two expensive rosters; the other six retain their rejected build and minimum car. Its production mean advantage (−1.146s for economical) is conditional on this cheap provisional menu, grid, two circuits and fixed race policy; it is not proof of a universal optimal strategy.

A broad library is not automatically used or better. In the synthetic economical bundle it costs35.25 development credits on average versus16.75 for small-library, completes2–3 packages versus1, yet its mean paired advantage is slightly smaller (−1.568s versus−1.584s). Two of16 large/small bundle-seed comparisons have exactly the same eventwise pace deltas despite different spending. Expensive large-library is slower in six of seven comparable seeds (mean+0.292s), while unaffordable small-library retains credits. These fixed schedules do not adapt failed early intents into later retries; actual sizes and failures matter.

Refinement is also conditional: economical mean+0.066s is slightly slower than Save despite spending16 credits on average; five of eight early parent purchases fail and later opportunities cannot refine a free substitute. Rush never guarantees a better result: economical synthetic Rush is slower in five comparable seeds, although its two faster seeds make its mean negative; expensive Rush is slower in three. The standard/Rush project draws are paired, but differing affordability changes which projects can actually be committed. These are failed/inconclusive policy hypotheses, not estimates of the probability distribution from eight seeds.

Production projects contain13 normal,1 partial and2 compromised logical outcomes, with zero observed breakthrough/delay and zero retirements; creation aliases repeat the same saved outcomes. The synthetic scopes contain66 normal,14 partial,16 breakthrough,9 compromised and10 delayed outcomes. Four permanent projects remain pending beyond the final preparation; other delays complete without repayment. Deterministic product tests, rather than seed search, establish every category and pending rejection.

Each synthetic arm/bundle group records20 whole-field retirements across its48 event-races, including one target retirement; those correlated counts are not new independent events across sibling policies. All14 synthetic groups retain one non-comparable cumulative two-car pair. No production target group begins leading or tied; synthetic groups expose both leading/trailing cases and zero tied cases. Actual original-leader final margin deltas versus the identical Save sibling range−6..0 points in production and−15..+51 in synthetic, retaining negative, positive and zero observations. Different Base Car inputs can change the actual first-event leader, whose identity is saved for that row. Short horizons and these conditional groups cannot establish runaway safety.
