## Findings, including limits

- **Circuit fit reverses.** In mixed noisy fields Speed averages about5.145s faster than Balanced at Monza, while it is0.234s slower at Monaco. Downforce averages5.189s slower at Monza and0.243s faster at Monaco. These are target-team mean deltas with the fixed grid/plan, not predicted race results for players.
- **Mechanical has no universal upgrade.** Both endpoints lose to Balanced on average in these cases. Mixed Monza Responsive averages+0.250s with SD0.130s, range−0.049 to+0.532s: one of12 seeds reverses the clear-control sign. Preserve this contradictory seed rather than selecting only wins. Several Speed deltas have SD below0.001s despite nonzero noise: common scoped paired randomness largely cancels; printed0.000 is rounding, not proof of zero uncertainty. Raw precision is retained.
- **Tyre compromise depends on the stint.** [Isolation probes](simulation/isolation-probes.json) retain complete full fields for one-lap and20-lap no-stop controls. On Monza, Warm-Up finishes the first lap0.192s ahead of Balanced; Preservation is0.192s behind. Across20 uninterrupted Medium laps, Preservation finishes0.081s ahead of Balanced and retains condition0.51555 versus0.51100; Warm-Up finishes0.089s behind with0.50637. Monaco shows the same direction. These no-stop probes correctly record ineligible two-compound usage and are not official race strategies. In the eligible20-lap one-stop comparison, Warm-Up wins and Preservation loses: the second warm-up cost outweighs its stint saving. Public event times show first post-pit Hard laps90.464/90.752/91.040s for Warm-Up/Balanced/Preservation in the clear mixed controls; the measure excludes pit loss. No universally best tyre choice is claimed.
- **Adaptability and information are distinct.** Authored opposing Responsive/Stable preferences with the shared Stable setup give the mismatched first driver compatibility8 at Adaptability10 and76 at95; the matched second driver remains100. The first driver's20-lap clear finish improves1794.083→1792.039s while Race Pace itself stays fixed. At either Adaptability value, raising engineer Setup and driver Setup Feedback10→95 changes Low→High confidence and reported uncertainty21→4 points, while the complete22-car race outcome hashes are identical.
